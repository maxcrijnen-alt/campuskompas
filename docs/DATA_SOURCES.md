## Required campus validation

Validate every room entrance, corridor, stair/lift stop, connector, outside crossing and accessible door. Confirm wheelchair clearances, lift access and opening restrictions. Calibrate graph coordinates against the original plans before connecting the route engine to them. Validate room existence and use, especially F3.025. Review weekly hours and exceptions; set exception coverage end date before enabling live status. There are no measured distances or estimated walking times in the initial dataset.

Demo community tips are conspicuously labelled examples, have zero fabricated votes, and do not claim to be real student submissions. Production community content comes only from approved records.
